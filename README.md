# Mood-Bar
A website where you track your mood with small notes on it. Pick as many jars as you want, the mood gets tracked daily, you can even share it with a friend!

## WHAT IS MOOD BAR?
-> mood bar is a website where you can throw in balls and write down a feeling alongside it,it has total of 6 emotions that cover every other emotion and tracks your emotions daily.

## PURPOSE: 
I made it for myself and those who dont have anyone to vent or share their happiness with. For example a person can pour their anger, their disghust, their happines into the jars and filld it up till their heart desires. It is not stopping anyone from venting but rather its a mood bar for fun and just fune purely.
